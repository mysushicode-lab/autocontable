## 🎬 Video Production Checklist

### For Professional Videos:

- [ ] Use french voice-over (female for empathy, clear diction)
- [ ] Shoot in 9:16 vertical format (mobile first)
- [ ] Keep text on screen > 3 seconds (reading time)
- [ ] Use green color for positive signals (compliance, success)
- [ ] Hook in first 1-2 seconds (stopping power)
- [ ] Include clear CTA at end (button + text)
- [ ] No music copyright issues (use Artlist/Epidemic Sound)
- [ ] Subtitles for viewers with sound off
- [ ] Test on mobile device (where 90% watch)

### Stock Resources:

- **Images:** Unsplash, Pexels (free)
- **Icons:** Feather Icons, Material Icons
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound, Artlist (subscription)
- **Voice-over:** Fiverr, Rev.com, Synthesia AI

## 📋 Copy Guidelines

### Do's ✅
- Use green checkmarks for benefits
- Use urgency language (limited time, only 7 days, etc.)
- Mention "free trial" prominently
- Use social proof (500+ businesses)
- Include French language
- Be specific (hours, percentage, concrete numbers)
- Add CTAs that are clear (Learn More, Sign Up)

### Don'ts ❌
- NO arrows pointing at button (Meta rejects)
- NO fake countdown timers
- NO cursor/mouse graphics
- NO excessive punctuation (!!!!)
- NO price in headline (for free tier)
- NO misleading claims
- NO competitor names

### Tone
- Professional but approachable
- French (France) language
- B2B (speaking to business owners)
- Solution-focused (not problem-heavy)
- Action-oriented (clear next steps)

## 📱 Mobile vs. Desktop Optimization

**Mobile (90% of traffic):**
- Vertical video (9:16)
- Large text (readable on 4-5" screen)
- Single benefit per section
- CTAs at bottom

**Desktop (10% of traffic):**
- Landscape optional (16:9)
- Smaller text OK
- More dense info
- CTAs on right

**Meta automatically optimizes** — just provide original in 9:16 and let Meta handle desktop version.
